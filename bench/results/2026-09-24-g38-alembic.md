# G-38: `alembic check` is green — the models describe the database that exists — 2026-09-24

## Finding

`alembic check` against a database at revision 0012 reported **45 upgrade operations**, so the
migration chain and the ORM had been two descriptions of the schema and the check was permanently
red (nobody could tell a real drift from the noise). Classified from the dump:

| ops | kind | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | `add_index ix_<table>_id` on primary keys | models' fault: `index=True` on a PK asks for a second, redundant index no migration created |
| 6 | `add_index` on `users.email` (unique), `users.username` (unique), `user_sessions.session_id` (unique), `user_sessions.user_id`, `user_sessions.device_token`, `exercise_templates.name` | **real**: declared by the models since the start, never created; the application relied on uniqueness the DB did not enforce |
| 10 | `remove_index` on `ix_form_checks_{user_id,video_id,status,exercise_type,created_at,status_updated_at}`, `ix_feedback_items_form_check_id`, `ix_videos_{status,user_id}`, `uq_form_checks_user_content_model_spec` (partial unique, the 0009 idempotency key) | models' fault: the DB has carried them since 0002–0009; no model declared them, so autogenerate wanted to **drop the idempotency key** |
| 1 | `remove_constraint uq_users_social_provider_social_id` | models' fault: undeclared |
| 16 | `modify_comment` on `videos.*` | real, cosmetic: the model has column comments, the DB does not |

## Change (`audit/g38-alembic`)

- Models (10 files, +37/−21): `index=True` removed from 11 primary-key declarations; `index=True`
  added on `form_checks.{user_id,video_id,status,exercise_type,created_at}`, `feedback_items.form_check_id`,
  `videos.{user_id,status}`; `FormCheck.__table_args__` declares `ix_form_checks_status_updated_at`
  and the partial unique `uq_form_checks_user_content_model_spec` (`postgresql_where=text("content_hash IS NOT NULL")`,
  matching `pg_get_indexdef`); `User.__table_args__` declares `uq_users_social_provider_social_id`.
  After this, `alembic check` reports exactly the 22 real operations (6 indexes, 16 comments).
- `migrations/versions/0013_reconcile_schema.py`: autogenerated for those 22, renamed to the repo's
  convention, hand-reviewed (docstring records the review), applied, and round-tripped.
- CI (`.github/workflows/backend-ci.yml`): `alembic upgrade head && alembic check` against the job's
  Postgres service, right after the image check, so the next drift fails the build.

## What this does not claim

- No query got faster: the six new indexes are on small tables (users, sessions, templates) and
  exist for correctness (uniqueness) and to match the models, not for a measured plan. G-01's
  "25 % query optimisation" stays withdrawn.
- G-23 (the app's `create_all()` at startup) is untouched; it is why the first boot of an empty
  database still bypasses Alembic. It is a separate branch.
